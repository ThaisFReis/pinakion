# Deliberation minutes

**A fictional case, for a demonstration of Pinakion.**

Filed by a juror, from their seat account. It carries no name: the name appears only when the jurors reveal themselves, after the verdict.

- The panel read the complaint, the evidence and the defence.
- On the facts: the review excerpts are in the history and the defence itself acknowledges the tone. The comment from the call is recorded in the community minutes.
- On the sanction: the defence asks for level 2. The case was opened at level 3, which is the ceiling: the panel can decide up to the loss of the role, not beyond.
- The vote is secret, through Tessera. These minutes do not say how anyone voted.
