01 / The guarantee

Three sentences.
Each with its own mechanism.

  1. 01

    Nobody chooses who judges you

    The roster is a classic Stellar asset with a locked issuer. The lock freezes the roster and fixes the drand round from the ledger clock. The seal verifies that round on chain, once and only once.

  2. 02

    Nobody knows who until they have judged

    Each juror keeps a secret. Whoever falls below the threshold proves the seat in zero knowledge, from a fresh account. The ledger sees a number, never a name.

  3. 03

    Anyone checks afterwards

    After the verdict, each juror reveals their secret. Anyone recomputes the panel from the events and drand alone, among those who revealed. Whoever did not reveal shows as silent.

Before you rely on it

The circuit’s setup was run by a single party: whoever ran it could forge seats. For the name not to appear, the seat account has to be funded with no trail back to the juror, and the relayer for that is not built. Whoever loses the secret loses the seat. Deliberation off chain breaks the seal as soon as the jurors meet. The vote depends on Tessera and its secrecy floor: below five votes there is no score. Testnet only, and nothing has been audited.

In development. The case, from the roster to the verdict, is built and has run on testnet. Roster renewal, contestation windows, a redraw after an adjournment and confidential case files are designed and not built.

02 / On testnet, 26 Oct 2026

A whole case,
from complaint to verdict.

A fictional code-of-conduct case, with real transactions. The person judged was on the roster and was impeded by the lock itself. The vote is secret and runs on Tessera, the same author’s earlier voting module, used as it is. One machine played every person.

  1. 40 → 7

    A sealed draw

    Forty people on the roster, seven seats at level 3. Every seat proven in zero knowledge, from a fresh account.

  2. 6 / 5

    Quorum and the record

    Six jurors attended from their seat accounts, without saying who they were. Complaint, evidence, defence and minutes were filed by their hashes.

  3. 5 · 1

    Verdict

    The contract accepted the vote only because Tessera’s voters were exactly the attendees. Upheld, with 4 of 7 votes required.

Read the recomputation method

The recipe is implemented in sorteio.mjs, with every source logged in the sources notebook.

  1. HMAC-SHA256(salt, seed) derives the key.
  2. ChaCha20Rng produces the sequence the shuffle consumes.
  3. Fisher-Yates sets the order; the panel takes the first positions.

The example on this page is round 33382274, drawn by contract CBPHNZLGQIUEC4BEC53ZP7P65LLIBT5JTWTHMZVAMCGZCEN3PSUHTPQN, which carries the beacon key pinned as a constant. Every hash is in the evidence record.
