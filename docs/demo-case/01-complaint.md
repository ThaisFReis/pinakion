# Complaint

**A fictional case, for a demonstration of Pinakion. No person, project or event here is real.**

**Project:** Agora, an open-source library with a code of conduct.
**Against:** one of the project's maintainers, identified in the contract only by their address.
**Level requested:** 3, loss of the maintainer role for six months.

## What happened

Between August and October, in reviews of pull requests from first-time contributors, the maintainer:

1. answered basic questions with irony and contempt ("this is in the documentation, which apparently nobody reads");
2. closed three contributions without explanation, after asking for changes that were then made;
3. made a comment about a person's accent during an open community call.

## What the code of conduct says

The project's code of conduct forbids "disparaging comments" and asks for "a welcome to newcomers". Level 3 of the project's scale provides for the temporary loss of a role.

## What is requested

That a panel drawn from the community's roster decide whether the conduct took place and whether the loss of the role for six months is warranted. The person filing the complaint asks that their name be kept out of the case record.
