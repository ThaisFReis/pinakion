      What this case does not show

      One machine played every person, and every account came from friendbot: in real life the seat account has to be funded with no trail back to the juror, and the relayer for that is not built. The circuit’s setup was run by a single party. The case documents are public here; a real case needs confidential files with justified access, which are designed and not built. Deliberation off chain breaks the seal as soon as the jurors meet. The windows are short so this fits a demonstration: the approved reveal floor is 30 days. The score’s correctness is checked by Tessera’s own verifier, not by this page. Testnet only, and nothing has been audited.
